# Indian Union Budget Data Analysis 🇮🇳📊

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring India's Union Budget — tracking how revenue is raised, how expenditure is allocated across ministries and sectors, and how fiscal deficit and key budgetary trends have evolved over time.

## 🎯 Objective
To analyze Union Budget data across multiple years and answer questions like:
- Which ministries/sectors receive the largest share of allocation, and how has that changed?
- How does revenue receipts (tax vs non-tax) compare to total expenditure over time?
- What is the trend in fiscal deficit, revenue deficit, and capital expenditure?
- How does budget allocation correlate with sector-specific outcomes (e.g. education, health, defence spend as % of GDP)?
